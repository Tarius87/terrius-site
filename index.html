01 · Game

Purpose

TriviSpar is a player-made trivia game: write a question for your opponent, they answer against a 20-second clock, and the AI referee holds the answer key and grades it fairly. Play 1v1 or party up to 16 players — in the same room or turn-by-turn from anywhere.

Development stage: In development.
